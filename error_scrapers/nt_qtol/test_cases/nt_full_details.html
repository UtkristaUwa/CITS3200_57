Request for Feedback - Provision of Network Management Services

The Department of Corporate and Digital Development (DCDD) is undertaking planning activities to inform the future replacement of the Northern Territory Government's Network Management Services contract. As part of this process, DCDD is seeking feedback from industry to better understand current market capability, emerging technologies, contemporary service delivery approaches and commercial models that may assist in shaping the future procurement strategy.
