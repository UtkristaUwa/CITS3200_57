Provision of Mobile Devices and Accessories - GSHS9477

27 Days until close

Description

Establish supply arrangements for mobile devices and accessories to support the operational and business needs of Department of Customer Service (DCS).

Project goals

The project aims to establish supply arrangements for mobile devices and accessories that enable DCS to meet its business and operational needs.

Through this procurement, DCS seeks to achieve competitive and transparent pricing, improved order fulfilment and delivery performance, enhanced customer and end-user experience, effective warranty and support services, and greater operational efficiency through accurate reporting and cost allocation.

Scope

DCS is seeking responses from suitably qualified suppliers for the provision of mobile devices and accessories.

Goods and services may include:

  • Mobile handsets, tablets, Wi-Fi hotspots and accessories
  • Device supply, fulfilment and delivery services
  • Warranty, repair and replacement services
  • Device enrolment services (Apple Business Manager and Samsung Knox)
  • Reporting and invoicing services
  • e-Waste management and device buy-back services

The procurement may result in the engagement of multiple suppliers to deliver the goods and services from February 2027 to February 2029, with extension options available.
