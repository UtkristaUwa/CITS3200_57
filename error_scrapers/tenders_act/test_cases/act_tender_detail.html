Public Housing Disability Modification and Domestic Violence Upgrade Services Multi Use List (MUL)
Directorate/Agency ACT Government
Type:
Other Arrangements
Status:
Open
Number:
PIMA0014211
UNSPSC:
72000000 - Building and Facility Construction and Maintenance Services
Note: this approach to market is Territory Funded Work covered by Secure Local Jobs Code.
Region(s):
Australian Capital Territory

Description

The Territory invites suppliers to submit an Application for the Public Housing Disability Modifications and Domestic Violence Modification Services MUL.

The purpose of this procurement is to establish a fit for purpose MUL for sourcing disability modification and domestic and family violence modification services for public housing properties.

The MUL will establish a qualified pool of suppliers that meet the Territory’s technical, licensing, insurance, safety, WHS and public housing specific requirements.

The MUL will be established for an initial period of one (1) years , with an optional extension period of one (1) years.  The MUL will remain open for receiving Applications from the date of publication until the MUL is formally closed by the Territory.

Suppliers may submit questions or seek clarification regarding the application process while the MUL remains open for Applications
