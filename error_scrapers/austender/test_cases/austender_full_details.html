    The Mid-Term Review (MTR) of the Western Province Partnership (WPP) will assess the Program's progress toward its intended outcomes, identify key achievements and challenges, and provide actionable recommendations to inform management decisions and program improvement and consolidation opportunities for the remainder of WPP Phase II. The MTR will assess the relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, sustainability and cross-cutting performance (including GEDSI, subnational governance and climate resilience) of WPP’s approach and delivery.
