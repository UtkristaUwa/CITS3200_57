About this Site

This site provides information about Tasmanian Government procurement opportunities for businesses. Registered businesses can download tender documents, lodge tender submissions online and receive email notification of new tenders open for bid that might be of interest to them.

Confidentiality in Government Contracts

Information relating to purchase orders over $2 million arising from a panel contract arrangement.
