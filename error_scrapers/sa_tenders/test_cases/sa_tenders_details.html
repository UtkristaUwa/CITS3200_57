Description

eProjects Panel

The eProjects Panel is for suppliers to engage with SA Government agencies for ICT-related Projects up to the value of $700,000 (GST Incl).

The eProjects Panel scope of projects include:

·          System development/deployment
·          System integration
·          Professional process services
·          Other information and technology projects

Cyber Security Services Panel

The Cyber Security Services Panel is for suppliers to engage with SA Government agencies for specialised Cyber-related Engagements up to the value of $700,000 (GST Incl).

The Cyber Security Services Panel scope of engagements include:

·          Investigation services
·          Security assessing
·          Security auditing
·          Security policy/strategy consultation
·          Security architecture and design
·          Security system development/analysis
·          Government security implementation

Further information about the Panels can be viewed at https://eprojects.sa.gov.au.

Notice Regarding Artificial Intelligence (AI) Services
Artificial Intelligence (AI) services are excluded from the scope of the eProjects Panels.  This exclusion includes AI model development, AI training, deployment activities, and AI-enabled platforms or services.
The current eProjects agreements were not designed with the contractual safeguards necessary to manage AI-specific risks, including privacy, security, data governance, intellectual property, transparency, ethical considerations, and ongoing risk management obligations.
The Department of Treasury and Finance will continue to monitor developments and assess whether future updates to the eProjects agreements are required to support AI services.

Supplier Application Process

Suppliers must review the attached eProjects Panel Agreement to ensure they can comply with all terms and conditions before accessing the Online application form.  In addition to this, each supplier is required to complete and submit either the eProjects - Form B - Supplier Statement and/or Cyber Security - Form C - Supplier Statement as part of the application process.

How to apply:

-           Review the attached ‘e Projects Panel Agreement v5.5’.
-           Download the required ‘eProjects - Form B - Supplier Statement v3.4’ and/or ‘Cyber Security - Form C - Supplier Statement v 3.3’ and complete ready for upload.
-           Access the correct eForm link for the panel you wish to apply for:

eProjects Panel(s) Online Application Form

Cyber Security Online Application Form

-           Complete all required sections of the online form accurately.  Mandatory fields are marked with * and must be filled in.
-           Attach all supporting documentation as specified in the form.  Incomplete submissions may not be assessed.
-           Submit your application only via the eForm process.  Applications sent via email or uploaded elsewhere will not be accepted.
-           Await confirmation via email once your application has been assessed.
-           Once assessed the outcome of your application will be communicated via email.

Existing Panel suppliers do not need to reapply.
