WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    CQUniversity is undertaking the consolidation of its TAFE operations into the Rockhampton North Campus as part of the University's broader campus redevelopment program. This project includes the construction of new facilities to support the expanded educational footprint.
    To facilitate these works, the existing fleet vehicle car park is required to be relocated to accommodate the proposed building footprint.
    This Request for Quote (RFQ) seeks submissions from suitably qualified contractors to undertake the construction of a new fleet vehicle car park, including all associated civil, security, and accessibility works necessary to deliver a complete and operational facility.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    ADDITIONAL ASSOCIATED WORKS
    Security fencing
    The contactor will be required to supply and install a new 2100mm high spear-top fence around the entire car park, excluding the entry driveway, equivalent to the Queensland Department of Education minimum requirements for construction and installation of school security fencing.
    Security lighting
    The Contractor will be required to supply and install security lighting to illuminate the car park for after-hours access.
    CQU would ideally like to use solar type lighting that provides a minimum of 100lux for the car park with a dimming feature to 30% of total lux when no movement is detected.
