Description
The search is on for an experienced hospitality operator to establish and run a new café at one of Victoria's most spectacular coastal destinations, with tenders open for the new Point Grey café in Lorne.
Located within the popular Point Grey precinct overlooking Louttit Bay and the iconic pier, the new café presents a unique opportunity for a business to become part of one of the Great Ocean Road's most visited tourism destinations.
The café will enhance the visitor experience at Point Grey, providing locals and visitors with a place to relax and enjoy refreshments while exploring, fishing or just taking in panoramic ocean views.
The café is currently being constructed in the new CoOp building and forms part of the Point Grey redevelopment project that will revitalising the whole precinct, including new landscaping, amenities and visitor experiences.
The successful operator will have the opportunity to activate the café space and provide a welcoming destination for visitors year-round, supporting tourism and creating local employment opportunities.
The tender process is open to hospitality businesses with a demonstrated capacity to deliver high-quality food, beverage and customer service offerings.
Interested parties can access tender documentation and application details via the Tenders Victoria Portal. Site inspections and briefing sessions will be available as part of the tender process.
