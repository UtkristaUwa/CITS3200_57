Description
Systems Assurance – Sourcing of Financial Statement IT Audit Services for up to Three-Years (Financial Year Ending 30 June 2027 to 30 June 2029) – Multiple Audits
1. Introduction
This Expression of Interest (EOI) invites suitably qualified IT Audit Service Providers (ITASPs) to register their interest to support VAGO in delivering financial statement IT audit services for multiple audits for up to three years from FY2027 to FY2029. For the purpose of this EOI, ITASPs may include existing VAGO Audit Service Providers engaged under the VAGO Financial Audit Service Provider Agreement or other IT Audit Service Providers willing to be engaged in accordance with applicable VAGO procurement requirements.
2. Background, Context and Purpose
VAGO currently delivers IT audit services across a number of financial audits annually using a combination of in-house Systems Assurance resources and external IT Audit Service Providers. This EOI relates to the approximate annual hours that may be delivered by successful ITASPs under future ITASP arrangements.
The future audit volumes and resourcing requirements may change due to audit and resourcing planning. Accordingly, VAGO is seeking a flexible sourcing approach that can respond to business requirements across FY2027 to FY2029.
The purpose of this EOI is to identify ITASPs that have the capability, capacity and interest to participate in a subsequent Request for Quotation (RFQ) process for eligible FY2027 – FY2029 financial statement IT audits.
The EOI is not a request for detailed pricing and does not guarantee that any provider will be invited to quote or awarded work.
Services include planning, interim and year-end IT audit procedures in support of financial statement audits, performed in accordance with VAGO's audit methodology and the requirements of ASA 315, including understanding the audit environment, identifying and evaluating IT-related risks, designing appropriate audit responses, and effectively integrating with Financial Audit (FA) teams to support systems assurance optimisation initiatives.
3. High Level Scope of Services
Services include planning, interim and year-end IT audit procedures in support of financial statement audits, performed in accordance with VAGO's audit methodology and the requirements of ASA 315, including understanding the audit environment, identifying and evaluating IT-related risks, designing appropriate audit responses, and effectively integrating with Financial Audit (FA) teams to support systems assurance optimisation initiatives.
4. Mandatory Requirements
Public liability and professional indemnity insurance to cover liabilities in aggregate amounting to a minimum of $10,000,000.
Ability to comply with VAGO’s confidentiality, privacy, data protection and intellectual property requirements.
Ability to satisfy VAGO’s independence and conflict of interest requirements for both the firm and proposed resources.
Ability to comply with applicable Victorian Government procurement requirements, including Local Jobs First Policy where applicable.
All proposed resources must be employees of the supplier.
No subcontracting or third-party engagement is permitted for delivery of services.
All proposed resources must be able to satisfactorily complete relevant criminal checks.
Use of VAGO systems, methodology, templates and quality requirements.
Operate under VAGO oversight and direction.
Willingness to participate in subsequent RFQ processes.
5. Indicative Resourcing Requirements
Based on past experience, our preferred resourcing model is outlined below; however, we welcome alternative approaches proposed by providers.
1 x IT Audit Director/Partner-level resource
2 × IT Audit Manager-level resources with CISA certification and a minimum of 3 years' recent Financial IT Audit Manager level experience
3 × IT Auditor-level resources with a minimum of 12 months' recent Financial IT Audit Team Member level experience
Providers should be able to provide backup and replacement resources where required, to deliver the assigned audit portfolio.
6. Indicative Volume and Timing Requirements
Approximate annual requirement: Approximately 2,000 hours across ~22 audits with different levels of scope and involvement on each audit.
Potential annual range: Initially estimated at 2,000 hours, with potential increases depending on audit volumes and approved extensions.
Contract structure: Initial one-year term with two optional one-year extensions at VAGO’s discretion and subject to approvals and business requirements.
Indicative annual delivery cycle: 
Planning: mid-October to pre-Christmas shutdown
Interim: post-Christmas to June
Year-end: July to August
Indicative effort profile:
Year-end: Approximately 20%
Planning: 20–30%
Interim: 50–60%

8. Assessment of EOI Responses
EOI responses will be reviewed to determine suitable providers for a subsequent RFQ process. The review may consider mandatory compliance, resource capacity, availability and flexibility, audit portfolio coverage, independence and conflict of interest considerations, and interest in contract structure.
9. Subsequent RFQ Process
Following assessment of EOI responses, VAGO may issue an RFQ to suitable providers for selected eligible financial statement IT Audits.
10. Conditions of EOI
This EOI does not constitute an offer, contract or commitment by VAGO to procure services.
VAGO may amend, suspend or discontinue the EOI process at any time.
Submission of EOI does not guarantee participation in a future RFQ process.
VAGO may request clarification, additional information or supporting evidence from respondents.
All information provided must be accurate, complete and authorised by the respondent.
Respondents are responsible for their own costs in preparing and submitting an EOI response.
