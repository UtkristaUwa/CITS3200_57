WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of the Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation invites Offers from eligible Suppliers for the provision of services to support the Western Queensland Surface Trough and Associated Rainfall and Flooding, Catchment Rehabilitation Program.
    The scope of this Invitation to Offer (ITO) focusses on the delivery of projects across 40 activated local government areas to support recovery, improve condition and build resilience in the impacted environments following the 2025 Western Queensland surface trough severe weather event.
    Eligibility - Please note that Suppliers and projects offered through this ITO process are required to meet eligibility requirements outlined within Part A - Invitation to Offer, Attachment C - Eligibility Details. Please ensure to carefully review this information prior to submitting your offer.
    Supplier Briefing Session - A Supplier Briefing Session will be held at 2.00pm on 11 August 2026 for this ITO. Please refer to Part A - Invitation to Offer, Section 1.5 - Briefing Session for details to register.
