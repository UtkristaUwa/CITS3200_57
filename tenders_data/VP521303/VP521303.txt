    Dear Nicole,
    We are preparing a response to CN-27466 and would be grateful for clarification on the following before we finalise our offer.
    1. Closing date
    The RFQ states a closing time of 2:00pm on Thursday 27 August 2026. VendorPanel VP521303 lists 2:00pm on Friday 28 August 2026. Could you please confirm which governs? We are working to the earlier date in the meantime.
    2. Mandatory criterion — Key Personnel (c)
    The third mandatory Key Personnel requirement is "previous experience with Queensland Department of Transport and Main Roads and Queensland Rail in the delivery of roads and/or rail infrastructure projects". We would appreciate confirmation on two points:
    (a) Whether experience with both agencies is required, or whether substantial experience with either is sufficient.
    (b) Whether experience gained delivering projects on TMR or Queensland Rail assets through a principal contractor, joint venture or alliance satisfies the requirement, or whether direct engagement by the agency is required. A significant proportion of senior rail delivery experience in Queensland is gained in that structure rather than through direct engagement, and a narrow reading would exclude candidates who have spent years delivering these agencies' projects.
    3. Basis of the 1,840 hours
    Evaluation Criteria 3 refers to availability "assumed at 1840 hours over contract term". Please confirm whether 1,840 hours is a maximum, an estimate for evaluation purposes, or a minimum commitment, and whether hours are drawn down against an approved forecast.
    4. Service location and site travel
    The supporting projects extend from Torbanlea to Varsity Lakes. Clause 8.2 of the General Contract Conditions reimburses travel only where the Customer requests the Supplier to travel away from the agreed service location. Could you confirm the intended service location for this engagement, and how travel to the supporting project sites is to be treated — as within the agreed service location and absorbed in the hourly rate, or as reimbursable travel requiring prior approval? This materially affects how we price.
    5. Price review over the extension option
    The Contract Details do not specify a price review mechanism, so under clause 8.3 of the General Contract Conditions the hourly rate would remain fixed across the initial term and the twelve-month extension option — a period of up to twenty-four months. Please confirm whether that is TMR's intention, or whether an escalation mechanism will apply to the extension.
    6. Framing of Evaluation Criteria 1
    Evaluation Criteria 1 seeks demonstrated experience undertaking comparable assignments and also requires a detailed CV for the nominated resource. Could you confirm whether responses should be framed around the nominated Key Personnel, the supplier organisation, or both?
    We are happy to discuss any of the above by telephone if that is easier.
    Kind regards,
    Ian
