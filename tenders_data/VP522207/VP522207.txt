WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    This Request for Tender invites suitably qualified Principal Contractors to submit a tender for two separate portions of works under a Design and Construct arrangement for a Specialist Clinic outpatient centre.
    The project aims to deliver a more compliant, functional and future-ready environment that supports both current and projected service needs.
    All works must align with the current design standards set out in the Australasian Health Facility Guidelines.
    The project will address a range of existing deficiencies, including:
    - a shortfall of compliant consult rooms
    - undefined and inadequate patient waiting areas
    - dysfunctional reception areas and workspaces
    - limited and poorly configured treatment spaces
    A mandatory site visit has been scheduled for 28 August 2026. Refer to the attached Request for Tender documents for further project details, including registration requirements for the site visit.
