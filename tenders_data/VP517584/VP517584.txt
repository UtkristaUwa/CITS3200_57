WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The tender comprises of two scopes, with separable portions, which University Sunshine Coast may elect to award to one supplier, or separate the scopes based on value delivery and program requirements. The tender is set out as:
    PART B.1 – BUILDING B REFURBISHMENT - 1 separable portion
    The University of the Sunshine Coast (UniSC) invites qualified contractors to tender for the delivery of the Building B Fresh Project at the Sippy Downs Campus under a Design and Construct contract. The project delivers a comprehensive program of internal and external refurbishment works to Building B - the University’s original and historically significant campus building.
    The successful supplier will be responsible for delivering the required works in accordance with UniSC requirements and the issued design documentation, within an operational university environment. The works will require effective coordination, staging and interface management, including alignment with early works delivered separately by UniSC. Further details of the scope, technical requirements, exclusions and contractual conditions are provided in Part B.1 of this Request for Tender.
    PART B.2 – BATHROOM AND OFFICE REFURBISHMENT - 4 separable portions
    The University of the Sunshine Coast (UniSC) wishes to invite qualified suppliers to provide design and construction services for the refurbishment and upgrade of selected bathroom amenities across Buildings I, K and Q1, and office facilities within Building I, at the Sippy Downs Campus.
    The scope of services includes the design, documentation, and delivery of refurbishment works to existing facilities, incorporating demolition, replacement of finishes and fixtures, minor reconfiguration where required, and associated building services upgrades. Works will focus on modernizing amenities and office environments while maintaining functionality and integrating with existing building infrastructure. Further details of the scope, technical requirements, exclusions and contractual conditions are provided in Part B.2 of this Request for Tender.
    There is a mandatory site information session on 10 August 2026 at 11:00am. Please register your attendance by email to Procurement@usc.edu.au by COB 6 August 2026.
