BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    This procurement will establish a Standing Offer Arrangement (SOA) for wound care consumables across Queensland Health, which comprises 16 Hospital and Health Services (HHSs) delivering care across the state.
    The SOA will consolidate two legacy arrangements, SOA274 (Wound Care Products) and SOA286 (Bandages, Surgical Sponges, Tapes, Surgical Pens, Scalpel Blades & Stitch Cutters), into a single statewide arrangement. Once established, it will be the required route for Hospital and Health Services to purchase in scope products under the Queensland Procurement Policy.
