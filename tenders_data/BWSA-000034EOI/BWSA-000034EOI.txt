Description
Barwon Water is seeking Expressions of Interest (EOI) from suitably qualified and experienced organisations to provide engineering, design, planning and program management services across its PS28 and PS33 capital program and support Barwon Water’s broader portfolio.
 This procurement is a two-stage process: EOI followed by Request For Tender (RFT), where only shortlisted Respondents from the EOI process will be invited to participate in the RFT.
