Description
The Shrine of Remembrance (the Shrine) is seeking to engage a professional and experienced Cleaning Services provider. The successful contractor will be responsible for delivering a comprehensive cleaning services program including, routine (regular after-hours), day-to-day (performed during normal business operating hours), periodic (planned deep cleaning) and reactive (ad-hoc) cleaning services across a minimum three-year period, with an option for a two-year extension.
The Shrine invites suppliers registered with the Victorian Government, Buying for Victoria Marketplace Suppliers List to submit a response to this RFT through the Buying for Victoria, Tenders Portal.
