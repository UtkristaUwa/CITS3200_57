WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details: Metro South Health is seeking a suitably qualified Principal Contractor to deliver the Logan Hospital Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT) Expansion project under a Design and Construct contract. The project involves reviewing and completing the existing 80% detailed design, refurbishment and expansion of the current ENT Department into adjacent clinical space, construction works, services integration, commissioning and handover within a live hospital environment. The expansion will increase clinical capacity, improve patient access and support growing demand for ENT, Audiology and associated specialist services.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details: The successful Contractor will be responsible for reviewing, validating, coordinating and completing the existing detailed design documentation, delivering the refurbishment and expansion works, managing all project interfaces and subcontractors, integrating required building services, and completing commissioning, defects management and handover. Works must be delivered in a staged manner within an operational hospital environment and in accordance with applicable legislation, standards and project requirements.
