Description
RFT-VSBA-USP-26/27 – Victorian School Building Authority (VSBA) – Unoccupied Site Maintenance and Associated Works Contract
The VSBA invites eligible respondents to submit a tender for the Unoccupied Sites Program.
This Request for Tender (RFT) follows the previous VSBA Forward Notice ATN-VSBA-USP-26/27-2.
Please refer to the Tender Notice and RFT documentation for more details.
Eligibility criteria
Respondents must be registered in the Construction Services – Project Management and/or Contract Management category on the Construction Supplier Register (CSR) with a maximum project value of at least $25 million.
Tender timelines
Enquiries must be lodged via online forum on www.tenders.vic.gov.au no later than 5:00PM on Monday, 14 September 2026 (local time in Melbourne).
Tender submissions must be lodged via www.tenders.vic.gov.au no later than 5:00PM on Thursday, 17 September 2026 (local time in Melbourne).
