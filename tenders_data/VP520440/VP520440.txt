WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details: This project comprises the replacement of 13 deteriorated culverts located between Peterson and Benaraby on the North Coast Line South corridor. The scope of works includes the full replacement of the existing concrete box culvert structures, removal and disposal of existing culverts and associated components, and construction of new culvert infrastructure, with all works to be completed with set closure periods. Construction to start in January 2027.
