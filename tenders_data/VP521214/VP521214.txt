WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR) invites suitably prequalified contractors to submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) for the Early Tenderer Involvement (ETI) of the Coomera Connector Stage 2 – Pimpama to Coomera project.
    The project involves the delivery of a new section of the Coomera Connector between Yawalpah Road to Shipper Drive.
    Tender documentation is available only to contractors prequalified with TMR at R5, B4, F150+, A4 or higher.
    Tenderers should note that the following requirements apply to this project:
    •	Building and Construction Code of Practice 2000 (Queensland Code)
    •	Queensland Charter for Local Content
    •	Australian Government Building and Construction Industry WHS Accreditation Scheme
    •	Queensland Procurement Policy (QPP) 2026
    To obtain access to the EOI documentation via QBuild (eTender), please contact Rosie Lonergan at projects.southcoast@tmr.qld.gov.au.
