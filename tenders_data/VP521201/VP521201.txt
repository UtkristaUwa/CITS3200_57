WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Package is being delivered through a partnership-based approach, working across Queensland Government, with up to six government partner agencies, to implement strategic funding partnerships. The Commission has established a group with representatives from each partner agency, to support coordination, collective impact measurement, shared learning and governance oversight across the package.
    The partnerships fund a range of initiatives including, a mix of place-based activities, community and local council grant programs, cross-sector collaboration, and activities that strengthen workforce capability, service integration, and policy and practice across systems. Activities are delivered across metropolitan, regional, and remote Queensland. Timing of initiatives will be staggered across financial years FY2025/2026 to mid FY2028/2029.
    The Commission requires an external Supplier to undertake an independent evaluation of the previous Grants and the Package, including development of an evaluation framework, to assess mental health and wellbeing outcomes and broader system impacts. The Supplier will be expected to design practical data collection and reporting approaches that partners can reasonably implement within existing operational environments.
    The evaluation will also examine the collaborative approach between the Commission and government partners to inform future policy and reform directions. The Supplier will be expected to take a collaborative and developmental evaluation approach, working alongside the Commission and government partners over the life of the Package to support learning, adaptation, and continuous improvement, rather than undertaking a single end-point evaluation.
    The evaluation is intended to inform future cross-government investment approaches, strengthen the evidence base for mental health promotion and prevention, and provide practical insights to inform ongoing mental health and wellbeing system reform and policy development in Queensland.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Queensland Mental Health Commission (the Commission) is a statutory body established under the Queensland Mental Health Commission Act 2013 to drive systemic reform across Queensland’s mental health, alcohol and other drugs, and suicide prevention systems. A core function of the Commission is to develop and facilitate implementation of a whole-of-government strategic plan to improve mental health and wellbeing outcomes for Queenslanders. The current plans, Shifting minds: The Queensland Mental Health, Alcohol and Other Drugs, and Suicide Prevention Strategic Plan 2023-2028 (Shifting minds) and its associated sub-plans; The Queensland Trauma Strategy 2024-2029, Achieving balance: The Queensland Alcohol and Other Drugs Plan 2022-2027 and Every life: The Queensland Suicide Prevention Plan 2019-2029 Phase Two, provides the overarching framework for this work.
    In addition, in response to Recommendation 19 of the 2022 Queensland Parliament Mental Health Select Committee Inquiry into opportunities to improve mental health outcomes for Queenslanders, Thriving Lives, Connected Communities – Queensland’s Commitment to Mental Health and Wellbeing (Thriving Lives, Connected Communities) was developed to provide a focused direction for improving community mental health and wellbeing.
    Recommendation 19 – Develop Mental Health and Wellbeing Strategy
    The committee recommends the Queensland Government considers the development of a population based Mental Health and Wellbeing Strategy that works across human services portfolios and is aimed at improving community mental health and wellbeing.
    Time-limited, non-recurrent funding has been provided to the Commission to progress priorities identified in Thriving Lives, Connected Communities.
    In 2025, the Commission delivered the Mental Health and Wellbeing Grants program, (the Grants) distributing $2.8 million across 22 projects to strengthen community mental health and wellbeing through mental health promotion, prevention and early intervention initiatives.
    Building on the Grants, the Commission established the Mental Health and Wellbeing Investment Package (the Package), a targeted, time-limited investment of $9 million that adopts a strategic and coordinated approach. The Package supports initiatives that strengthen population-based mental health and wellbeing through mental health promotion and prevention, while leveraging existing systems, infrastructure and partnerships to maximise impact, reduce duplication and support sustainability beyond the funding period.
