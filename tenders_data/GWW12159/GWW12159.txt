Description
Greater Western Water (GWW) invites the Tenderer to submit a Tender for the detailed design and construction of the Melton Recycled Water Plant Potable Water Service upgrade. The objective of the project is to upgrade and improve fire and hydraulic services as per Greater Western Water technical specification and relevant Australian safety standards and codes.
If you are interested with this opportunity, the tender information and submission requirement can be found at https://portal.tenderlink.com/greaterwesternwater with Notice number CWW-1170142 or tender title GWW12159 Design and Construct of Melton RWP Potable Water Services Upgrade.
A tender briefing will be provided on 2:00pm AEST 17 August 2026 (pre-booking via email is required).
Subsequently, Tenderers will have the opportunity to attend a site visit on 19 August 2026 (pre-booking via email is also required).
Please do not submit your response at Buying for Victoria website.
