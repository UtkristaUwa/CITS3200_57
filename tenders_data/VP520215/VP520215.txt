WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of Primary Industries (DPI), through Research Infrastructure, requires the provision of cleaning services for Bribie Island Research Facility.
    Cleaning tasks are completed on a scheduled basis, ranging from daily cleaning tasks to annual and on-demand activities. Tasks undertaken are grouped into five service categories:
    1.	Flooring,
    2.	Window Glass, Sills & Internal Walls, Ceilings
    3.	Toilets, washrooms, showers and locker rooms,
    4.	Kitchen and breakout areas, and
    5.	Office areas, hallways, lab rooms and furniture
    The Supplier will be required to supply the required consumables, plant and equipment to undertake the services. The initial contract term is till 31 October 2027; with two (2) 12-month extension options. The complete contract requirements are specified in Schedule 1 of the attached ‘Contract Details’.
    This RFQ has a mandatory industry briefing on 9:00am AEST Tuesday, 18 August 2026. Suppliers will meet at: 144 North Street, Woorim QLD 4507. Please register for the site visit via email no later than 2:00pm AEST, Friday, 14 August 2026 via procurement@dpi.qld.gov.au.

    Answered on 24/Aug/2026 02:36 PM

    Thank you for your question.
    The monthly fixed price is intended to cover all cleaning services classified as Routine in Appendix 2 – Cleaning Schedule, inclusive of labour, equipment, consumables, supervision, and overhead costs necessary to perform those routine tasks.
