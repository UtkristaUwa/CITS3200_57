Description
Nature Parks is seeking suitably qualified structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, engineering geology and infrastructure inspection Suppliers to undertake professional inspections, assessments and reporting across three Work Packages:
• Work Package 1 – Boardwalks and Pedestrian Structures, as defined in A3A.
• Work Package 2 – Buildings and Facility Structures, as defined in A3B.
• Work Package 3 – Non-Invasive Geotechnical and Coastal Hazard Inspections, as defined in A3C.
Tenderers may submit an Offer for any one Work Package or any combination. Each selected Work Package must be fully priced and resourced as a complete package. Nature Parks may award the Work Packages separately or together and is not required to award all Work Packages to the same Supplier.
A single set of common procurement and contract requirements applies to all Work Packages. Package-specific inspection methodology, technical requirements, asset and map-sheet schedules and deliverables are contained in A3A, A3B, A3C and A6.
