Description
Barwon Water is inviting tenders for Major Sewer Works packages in the Geelong region.
The Scope of Work comprises two rehabilitation packages:
Bellerine Street Ovoid Sewer Liner Rectification: Removal of the existing defective CIPP liner and installation of a new liner within approximately 400 m of the Bellerine Street Ovoid Sewer.
Major Gravity Sewer Rehabilitation: Rehabilitation of approximately 1 km of major gravity sewers across seven rail and road corridor locations, ranging in diameter from 300 mm to 1300 mm, including      sections of the Black Rock Outfall Sewer and Geelong Ovoid Sewer systems.
There will be a Mandatory Briefing session held on Monday 10th August online only. Please register with Guilliano Andy by emailing Guilliano.Andy@barwonwater.vic.gov.au by 3.00pm Friday 7th August.
