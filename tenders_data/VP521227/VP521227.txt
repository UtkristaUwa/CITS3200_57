BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department manages sporting facilities located in Queensland, Australia (Venues).
    The Venues has a large membership base over several locations and hosts casual members of the community including students with disabilities as well as athletes and visitors every year.
    The Venues include some of the following facilities, 20m indoor heated pool, 50m outdoor pool, recovery ice and heated baths, 600sqm open-air gym, (WA) accredited athletics track with turn infield, FIH standard hockey pitch and two (2) international standard beach volleyball courts. In addition, there are also lodges that can accommodate guests in twin and quad-share comfort, fully equipped, multi-purpose conference facilities, Allied Health Services and bistros catering for guests.
    The Venues house open-air and closed gyms providing a large range of bikes, rowing machines, fixed weight machines and free weighs to members and visitors.
    Local, state, national and international athletes, teams and sporting bodies will access the facilities including the gym Equipment as part of their training regime.
    Some of the Venues due to the nature of the open-air gymnasium may seek Equipment suited to this environment to minimise the effects of premature and accelerated ageing.
    The scope of the potential Contract will cover the following requirements (see Part B: Head Lease Agreement Details, Schedule 1 Requirements for full details):
    (a)	Provision of new Equipment under the Agreement including:
    a.	Cardio and Strength Equipment – Refer 26DSROPG025 - Attachment A;
    (b)	Delivery, installation and training of the above new leased Equipment.
    (c)	Delivery of planned and non-scheduled maintenance and repairs and replacements of parts as required.
    (d)	Removal of the leased equipment at the completion of the lease period.
    The following is out-of-scope for this Contract:
    (a)	Removal and disposal of current equipment.
