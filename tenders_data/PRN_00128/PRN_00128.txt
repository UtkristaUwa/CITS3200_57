Description
The opportunity
The Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA) invites suitably qualified and experienced suppliers to submit a proposal for the provision of Waste Management Services and Reporting across DEECA’s metropolitan and regional sites throughout Victoria.
Suppliers must demonstrate significant experience and capability in delivering comprehensive waste management services, including waste collection, disposal, resource recovery, recycling initiatives, data management, and reporting. Responses should outline the supplier’s approach to service delivery, continuous improvement, sustainability outcomes, and compliance with relevant legislative and regulatory requirements.
This RFT package outlines the requirements, conditions and information governing the procurement process, structured to provide a clear understanding of the scope, participation rules, timelines, contractual expectations and response format. The RFT documents include:
Part A – Request for Tender : Core RFT details and specification.
Part A.1 – About this Invitation: Key procurement information, including timelines, contact points and submission instructions.
Part A.2 – Statement of Requirements : Goods/services sought, expected outcomes and technical, operational and compliance obligations.
Part B – Conditions of Participation : Rules for participating, including probity, compliance and minimum standards.
Part C – Proposed Contract : Commercial, legal and performance conditions forming the basis of the final contract, to be updated following negotiation with the preferred Supplier.
Part D – Offer (Response Schedule) : Templates and forms Respondents must complete and return to ensure consistent and evaluable submissions.
Note to Tenderers: Only Tenderers capable of providing all of the Goods and/or Services and complying in full with the conditions set out in this RFT should submit a Tenderer’s Response
