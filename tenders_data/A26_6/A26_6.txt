A.A.2	The Requirement
The National Capital Authority (NCA) is seeking to engage a suitably qualified and experienced consultant for Independent Technical Advisor (ITA) services at Scrivener Dam. The services include regular audits to assess NCA’s compliance to the Dam Safety Management Plan, and the provision of independent technical advice for the dam safety management of Scrivener Dam. 
Scrivener Dam:
Scrivener Dam is located on the Molonglo River, in Canberra, and forms Lake Burley Griffin. It is an asset of National importance and is classified as a Large Dam with a 'High C' Consequence Category (in the Hydrology, Dam Break and Consequence Category assessment undertaken by SMEC in 2021) in accordance with the Australian National Committee on Large Dams (ANCOLD) definitions and guidelines.
Dam Safety Management Plan (DSMP):
The NCA does not currently come under the jurisdiction of a dam safety regulator within the Utilities Technical Regulator and has therefore implemented a self-regulated approach to dam safety. It has adopted a Dam Safety Code which specifies the actions to be taken to achieve this position. The NCA’s Dam Safety Code has been developed in consideration of contemporary good practice including the ACT Dam Safety Code.
In 2014, the ACT Government enacted the Utilities (Technical Regulation) Act 2014 and Utilities (Dam Safety Code) Determination 2014, with the intent of regulating all notifiable dams in the ACT. Legal advice was obtained through the Australian Government Solicitors in 2015 which confirmed that the ACT Dam Safety Code was not enforceable on the Commonwealth and therefore, Scrivener Dam. On 13 July 2015, the regulator acknowledged NCA’s intent on remaining self-regulated, but was including the dam in the ACT Dams Register as a listed dam.
The Dam Safety Management Plan (DSMP) is updated annually. The DSMP encompasses the Dam Safety Policy, Dam Safety Code and details actions, and responsibilities required to manage and operate Scrivener Dam in accordance with the relevant codes and guidelines. The hierarchy of the DSMP is illustrated below in Figure 1. The DSMP ensures that the NCA’s dam safety management of Scrivener Dam is in accordance with contemporary good practice, including guidelines set out by ANCOLD. 
Figure 1 – Hierarchy of the DSMP
General requirements: 
The successful Supplier will be suitably qualified and have sound knowledge of relevant ANCOLD and ICOLD guidelines, and Australian Standards as appropriate and will support contemporary good practice for dam safety management as applied to Scrivener Dam.
The successful Supplier will provide technical advice and support regarding dam safety management for Scrivener Dam. The ITA will also be responsible for the Annual Validation Audits and 5-yearly Functional Audit (due 2029) of the DSMP to assess the performance of the NCA against the requirements of the individual task assignments specified in the DSMP. There are currently (as of August 2025), twenty-three individual task assignments specified in the DSMP, included at ATTACHMENT A.  
The successful Supplier is to provide technical advice to review the following elements of the Plan and provide support when required: 
Dam Safety Management Policy

Dam Safety Governance
Dam Safety Culture
Industry Awareness within the NCA

Establish Dam Safety Requirements for Operations, Maintenance and Surveillance

Deliverables:
Annual Validation Audit of the DSMP
The Supplier will be required to undertake annual Validation Audits of the DSMP by 31 August each year to assess the performance of the NCA against the requirements of the DSMP and to confirm the required activities have been completed to an appropriate technical standard. A Validation Audit is an audit of technical quality and adequacy of each completed task. These audits must be undertaken by individuals with necessary technical skills to critically examine and review each task output against defined or industry standards. For example, this audit will consider key dam safety documents such as the Dam Safety Emergency Plan and will review that its content is consistent with that required by industry standards (ANCOLD Guidelines on Dam Safety Management), and that the technical details against each element of the Dam Safety Emergency Plan are sound and logical from an engineering and dam safety perspective. This review is general commenced in June each year and a draft report provided by July and finalised by 31 August each year. The NCA will provide nominated documents created during each financial year in a digital form for review.
Estimated Level of Effort:
To assist tenderers preparing their submissions, the Customer advises that, based on similar requirements, the estimated level of effort to complete the Annual Validation Audit is approx. 110 hours, including document review, stakeholder engagement, analysis, and reporting.
The actual level of effort may vary depending on the number, quality, and complexity of documents provided, as well as the personnel engaged, their experience, and the efficiency of the audit approach and methodology applied.
Functional Audit of the DSMP
Functional Audits are undertaken at least every 5 years or when there is a significant change in dam industry practice or if there is a significant change in the NCA’s operational environment. The last Functional Audit of the DSMP was completed in June 2024 and will be required during the Contract period in August 2029. These audits examine whether all necessary tasks are appropriately specified, including actual delivery requirements, timing and frequencies. They are designed to identify if any significant changes have occurred in either the individual operating environment or in the broader industry which would require a change in activities or details. For example, a Functional Audit would review all tasks, outputs, responsibilities, and frequencies outlined in this Dam Safety Management Plan and, in particular, the Responsibility Matrix and Individual Task Assignments (ATTACHMENT A). The Functional Audit includes an independent audit of the dam surveillance program as detailed below. The audit is to be undertaken in accordance with the requirements of ISO 19011:2018 Guidelines for Auditing Management Systems. 
Estimated Level of Effort:
To assist tenderers preparing their submissions, the Customer advises that, based on similar requirements, the estimated level of effort to complete the 5-yearly Functional + Annual Validation Audit is approx. 175 hours, reflecting its broader scope, system-level review, and increased volume and complexity of documentation.
The actual level of effort may vary depending on the number, quality, and complexity of documents assessed, as well as the personnel engaged, their experience, and the efficiency of the audit approach and methodology applied.
Independent Audit of the Dam Surveillance Program
The NCA is required to obtain an independent audit of the dam’s surveillance program every 10 years, as prescribed by Section 5.7 of the ANCOLD ‘Guidelines on Dam Safety Management’ (2003). The next independent audit of the dam’s surveillance program is scheduled to be completed in 2034, this audit is excluded from the scope of work.
Independent Technical advice
The supplier will be required to provide independent technical advice to the NCA regarding dam safety related matters. The cost for advice will be based on hourly rates specified in the Contract with deliverables and reporting agreed to with the NCA on a case-by-case basis.

A.A.2	The Requirement
The National Capital Authority (NCA) is seeking to engage a suitably qualified and experienced consultant for Independent Technical Advisor (ITA) services at Scrivener Dam. The services include regular audits to assess NCA’s compliance to the Dam Safety Management Plan, and the provision of independent technical advice for the dam safety management of Scrivener Dam. 
Scrivener Dam:
Scrivener Dam is located on the Molonglo River, in Canberra, and forms Lake Burley Griffin. It is an asset of National importance and is classified as a Large Dam with a 'High C' Consequence Category (in the Hydrology, Dam Break and Consequence Category assessment undertaken by SMEC in 2021) in accordance with the Australian National Committee on Large Dams (ANCOLD) definitions and guidelines.
Dam Safety Management Plan (DSMP):
The NCA does not currently come under the jurisdiction of a dam safety regulator within the Utilities Technical Regulator and has therefore implemented a self-regulated approach to dam safety. It has adopted a Dam Safety Code which specifies the actions to be taken to achieve this position. The NCA’s Dam Safety Code has been developed in consideration of contemporary good practice including the ACT Dam Safety Code.
In 2014, the ACT Government enacted the Utilities (Technical Regulation) Act 2014 and Utilities (Dam Safety Code) Determination 2014, with the intent of regulating all notifiable dams in the ACT. Legal advice was obtained through the Australian Government Solicitors in 2015 which confirmed that the ACT Dam Safety Code was not enforceable on the Commonwealth and therefore, Scrivener Dam. On 13 July 2015, the regulator acknowledged NCA’s intent on remaining self-regulated, but was including the dam in the ACT Dams Register as a listed dam.
The Dam Safety Management Plan (DSMP) is updated annually. The DSMP encompasses the Dam Safety Policy, Dam Safety Code and details actions, and responsibilities required to manage and operate Scrivener Dam in accordance with the relevant codes and guidelines. The hierarchy of the DSMP is illustrated below in Figure 1. The DSMP ensures that the NCA’s dam safety management of Scrivener Dam is in accordance with contemporary good practice, including guidelines set out by ANCOLD.

General requirements: 
The successful Supplier will be suitably qualified and have sound knowledge of relevant ANCOLD and ICOLD guidelines, and Australian Standards as appropriate and will support contemporary good practice for dam safety management as applied to Scrivener Dam.
The successful Supplier will provide technical advice and support regarding dam safety management for Scrivener Dam. The ITA will also be responsible for the Annual Validation Audits and 5-yearly Functional Audit (due 2029) of the DSMP to assess the performance of the NCA against the requirements of the individual task assignments specified in the DSMP. There are currently (as of August 2025), twenty-three individual task assignments specified in the DSMP, included at ATTACHMENT A.  
The successful Supplier is to provide technical advice to review the following elements of the Plan and provide support when required:

Deliverables:
Annual Validation Audit of the DSMP
The Supplier will be required to undertake annual Validation Audits of the DSMP by 31 August each year to assess the performance of the NCA against the requirements of the DSMP and to confirm the required activities have been completed to an appropriate technical standard. A Validation Audit is an audit of technical quality and adequacy of each completed task. These audits must be undertaken by individuals with necessary technical skills to critically examine and review each task output against defined or industry standards. For example, this audit will consider key dam safety documents such as the Dam Safety Emergency Plan and will review that its content is consistent with that required by industry standards (ANCOLD Guidelines on Dam Safety Management), and that the technical details against each element of the Dam Safety Emergency Plan are sound and logical from an engineering and dam safety perspective. This review is general commenced in June each year and a draft report provided by July and finalised by 31 August each year. The NCA will provide nominated documents created during each financial year in a digital form for review.
Estimated Level of Effort:
To assist tenderers preparing their submissions, the Customer advises that, based on similar requirements, the estimated level of effort to complete the Annual Validation Audit is approx. 110 hours, including document review, stakeholder engagement, analysis, and reporting.
The actual level of effort may vary depending on the number, quality, and complexity of documents provided, as well as the personnel engaged, their experience, and the efficiency of the audit approach and methodology applied.
Functional Audit of the DSMP
Functional Audits are undertaken at least every 5 years or when there is a significant change in dam industry practice or if there is a significant change in the NCA’s operational environment. The last Functional Audit of the DSMP was completed in June 2024 and will be required during the Contract period in August 2029. These audits examine whether all necessary tasks are appropriately specified, including actual delivery requirements, timing and frequencies. They are designed to identify if any significant changes have occurred in either the individual operating environment or in the broader industry which would require a change in activities or details. For example, a Functional Audit would review all tasks, outputs, responsibilities, and frequencies outlined in this Dam Safety Management Plan and, in particular, the Responsibility Matrix and Individual Task Assignments (ATTACHMENT A). The Functional Audit includes an independent audit of the dam surveillance program as detailed below. The audit is to be undertaken in accordance with the requirements of ISO 19011:2018 Guidelines for Auditing Management Systems. 
Estimated Level of Effort:
To assist tenderers preparing their submissions, the Customer advises that, based on similar requirements, the estimated level of effort to complete the 5-yearly Functional + Annual Validation Audit is approx. 175 hours, reflecting its broader scope, system-level review, and increased volume and complexity of documentation.
The actual level of effort may vary depending on the number, quality, and complexity of documents assessed, as well as the personnel engaged, their experience, and the efficiency of the audit approach and methodology applied.
Independent Audit of the Dam Surveillance Program
The NCA is required to obtain an independent audit of the dam’s surveillance program every 10 years, as prescribed by Section 5.7 of the ANCOLD ‘Guidelines on Dam Safety Management’ (2003). The next independent audit of the dam’s surveillance program is scheduled to be completed in 2034, this audit is excluded from the scope of work.
Independent Technical advice
The supplier will be required to provide independent technical advice to the NCA regarding dam safety related matters. The cost for advice will be based on hourly rates specified in the Contract with deliverables and reporting agreed to with the NCA on a case-by-case basis.
