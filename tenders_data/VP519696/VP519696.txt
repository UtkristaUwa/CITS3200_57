WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The objective of this project is to extend the operational life and improve the utilisation of the existing Maudsley Building at the Baillie Henderson Hospital through refurbishment and adaptive reuse, transforming the former mental health ward into a contemporary administrative workplace for Darling Downs Health.
    The project will serve as a pilot implementation of Adaptable Administrative Workspace Design Principles, providing an opportunity to evaluate activity-based working environments and inform future workplace planning initiatives across Darling Downs Health.
    The refurbishment will address identified building deficiencies, including ventilation, moisture management, compliance and amenity issues, while maximising the value of the existing asset and supporting increasing administrative accommodation demands associated with the transition to a single-campus model at Baillie Henderson Hospital and the planned opening of the New Toowoomba Hospital.
    Recognising that the Maudsley Building is identified for future demolition under the Baillie Henderson Hospital Master Plan, the refurbishment is intended to provide a fit-for-purpose administrative accommodation solution with an anticipated operational life of approximately 10 years, delivering a cost-effective interim outcome that meets Darling Downs Health's medium-term accommodation requirements.
    This Request for Tender seeks to engage a Principal Contractor for two separate portions under a Design and Construct arrangement.
    A mandatory site visit has been rescheduled to 17 August 2026. See attached Request for Tender documents for further details.
