Description
To seek tenders from suitable building contractors to construct alterations and additions to the Alexandra Fire Station.
Scope includes:
Demolition works to remove existing BA Room from inside existing Motor Room and relocation of existing COMMs alcove.
Construction of Extension to include Turnout Room, Change Cubicle, BA Washroom, Laundry and BA Compressor Fill Station Room.
Epoxy Flooring to all areas including the existing Motor Room.
