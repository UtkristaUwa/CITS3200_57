Multi Agency Access: Yes
Multi Agency Access Type: All Agencies
Panel Arrangement: No
Multi-stage: Yes
Multi-stage Criteria: From this ITR, a shortlist of applicants will be selected and invited to submit Tenders at the RFT stage commencing Sep 2026.
Description: 3FIR forms part of the Republic of Fiji Military Forces and provides land-based security capability for domestic operations and international deployments. This project involves the design of a new military headquarters precinct, comprising headquarters accommodation, supporting administration and operational facilities, associated infrastructure and external works at Queen Elizabeth Barracks, Suva. The project involves development of the complete suite of design services to deliver the Project through all nominated design stages.
Other Instructions: Two stage procurement process. Stage 2, Request for Tender, commencing in September 2026 for shortlisted applicants. Applicants may direct any questions pertaining to this procurement to 19 CE Works at the following email address: 19cew.p253.3fir@defence.gov.au
Conditions for Participation: Nil
Timeframe for Delivery: Estimated Design time 12 Months, Estimated delivery time 24 months

3.  
We seek clarification regarding the approach to 
delivery that would be preferred by the 
Commonwealth for this project. Specifically, we 
would appreciate guidance on whether a modular or 
prefabricated construction approach—developed 
and assembled in Australia and then transported for 
installation in Fiji—would be considered or 
potentially welcomed as part of a proposal. 
The Commonwealth has not prescribed a preferred 
delivery methodology at this ITR stage. Applicants 
are encouraged to identify any innovative and 
efficient approaches they consider appropriate for 
delivery of the Project. 
As part of the Preliminary Proposal requested in 
Schedule A to the Invitation to Register Interest, 
Applicants should describe (among other things) an 
approach to maximising opportunities for Fijian 
industry participation, workforce engagement, 
capability development, and sustainable economic 
outcomes and as otherwise described in that 
Schedule A.  Such information is to be provided for 
evaluation purposes. 
4.  
As the ITR documentation references maximising 
Fijian supply and local labour input, could you 
please clarify if the preference is strictly for local 
(in-country) delivery models and materials, or is 
there scope to propose a delivery model that 
incorporates prefabrication off-shore while still 
maximising opportunities for Fijian industry 
participation? 
The Commonwealth seeks to maximise 
opportunities for Fijian industry participation, 
workforce engagement, capability development and 
sustainable economic outcomes. Applicants should 
also have regard to the requirement to maximise the 
use of locally sourced materials where consistent 
with project requirements.  
The Commonwealth has not prescribed a specific 
delivery model at the ITR stage. Applicants may 
propose solutions that incorporate both local and 
offshore resources, materials or capabilities, 
provided they clearly demonstrate how their 
proposed approach maximises meaningful 
participation by Fijian industry and supports local 
economic outcomes and on the basis requested by 
Schedule A to the Invitation to Register Interest.
