Description
Ports Victoria will be seeking to engage services of a suitably qualified Contractor who specialises in Traffic Management Services (TMS) for Station Pier Operations, with relevant maritime experience.
The works will involve:
- TMS for normal Station Pier operations
- TMS for Cruise season operations
- TMS for surge and where required for Station Pier operations.
# Only Tenderers with the following accreditations and qualifications will be considered:
- that are able to demonstrate relevant experience in the implementation and supervision of traffic management arrangements within complex operational environments involving interactions between heavy vehicles, light vehicles, pedestrians and active site operations.
- with current Department of Transport and Planning accreditation for Traffic Management Implementation, Road Category 2 or higher;
# that have personnel with:
- current Victorian traffic management qualifications and authorisations appropriate to their respective roles;
- current Maritime Security Identification Act Compliance (MOTSFA) or satisfy the relevant escorted access requirements that are able to demonstrate compliance and experience with the DTP Temporary Traffic Management Accreditation Program, which specifically covers the design and implementation of temporary traffic management.
- the Victorian Code of Practice for Worksite Safety – Traffic Management
