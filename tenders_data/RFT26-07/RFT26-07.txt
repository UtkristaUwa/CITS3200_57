RFT 26-07 Weed control services
Greater Melbourne Cemeteries Trust (GMCT) invites Tenderers to submit a fixed lump sum price tender, in accordance with the Tender Documents contained within this Invitation for Tender for Weed Control Services.
The Greater Metropolitan Cemeteries Trust (GMCT) is seeking to establish a contract with a suitably skilled and experienced contractor (Contractor) for the provision of Weed Control Services (Services) for 18 sites located in the Melbourne Metro area with 1 future site coming online in 2027 in the western region.
The contract term will be for a three-year period, with the option of two (2) one-year extensions (Contract Term), with each option executed at the discretion of the GMCT.

For the duration of the term the supplier’s key responsibilities will be to:
·             Minimise the visual impact that weeds have on the amenity of GMCT’s sites
·             Ensure weed control across the GMCT sites is managed sustainably
·             Control invasive weeds in a safe manner in environmentally sensitive areas
·             Ensure the GMCT is providing well cared for and maintained landscapes for the community, preventing damage to infrastructure, which may occur if not adequately managed.
