WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    In response to disaster events - Queensland Monsoon Trough, Cyclone Koji, Cyclone Narelle and Severe Weather commencing 24 December 2025, the Community Development Program (the Program) is part of an exceptional circumstances package jointly funded by the Australian and Queensland governments.
    The Department of Families, Seniors, Disability Services and Child Safety is seeking offers from organisations to deliver the Financial Wellbeing and Social Services Sector Recovery elements of the Program to support the recovery and future resilience of communities impacted across Northern and Central Queensland; and Southern and Western Queensland, in identified Local Government Areas.
    Please see the attached Request for Quote and associated documents for more information on how to submit an offer.
