    Answered on 18/Aug/2026 02:29 PM
    :
    1. MEP TIMING
    The MEP is required within 10 business days of award of contract and at least 5 business days prior to commencement of dredging - whichever is the earliest.
    2. WEEKLY pH MONITORING
    The dredging Contractor must record pH levels as part of their water quality monitoring responsibilities (which include visual plumes and petroleum, scum and litter monitoring).
    GCWA's Water Quality Monitoring consultant must include recording of pH levels in their real-time telemetry at monitoring points.
    For clarity: the Contractor confirms pH levels adjacent to the dredge and the WQM consultant confirms pH levels adjacent to sensitive receptors.

    Question
    :
    Three Questions:
    -	South Channel Area B Dredge Depth. Design and maximum dredge depths are the same (-3.0m LAT; Part 5 Table 1). The corresponding design and maximum volumes in Table 2 are not the same. Can you confirm the maximum depth for South Channel Area B.
    -	Batter Tolerance. The design drawings (Part 5 Appendix B1 & B2) typical dredge section show the design and maximum dredge profile which includes a dredge batter that extends up from the maximum dredge depth. Part 8 Clause 12.3 states “Where the dredged depth exceeds the minimum depth, the line of the batter shall be extended downwards until it meets the actual dredged depth.” This description does not sound consistent with the batter tolerance shown in the drawings. Please confirm that the maximum profile shown in the drawing is correct.
    -	Coomera River Area F Methodology. Part 5 Table 2 indicates that bed levelling is the expected disposal option for Area F, but Price Schedule includes disposal at Narrowneck with bed levelling as “RATE ONLY”. Please clarify.
    Thank you
    Answered on 24/Aug/2026 12:25 PM
    :
    1 - SOUTH CHANNEL DEPTH
    SMP Table 2 is correct. RFT Part 5 Table 1 is incorrect.
    South Channel Area B design depth is -4.5m LAT +0.5m insurance = -5.0mLAT maximum payable dredge depth.
    2 - BATTER TOLERANCE
    Maximum volume calculations are based on the profile provided in the design drawings.
    The details specified in Part 8 Clause 12.3 aim to clarify that the batter must extend from the toe of the actual dredged depth.
    The latter is the required and expected configuration.
    3 - COOMERA AREA F
    Sorry for the confusion.
    Although deposition at Narrowneck is the preferred option, it is anticipated that the cost associated with removing only 269m³ to achieve the design depth would be disproportionate and not cost-effective. Accordingly, bed levelling is expected to be instructed as the more practical approach.
    However, the current scope is still for deposition at Narroweneck and the cost sheet must reflect this.
