Description
Ambulance Victoria (AV), an agency of the Department of Health & Human Services (DHHS), is the statutory provider of pre-hospital emergency health care and ambulance transport, non-urgent patient transport services for all members of the community as well as providing telehealth advice and support drawing upon our clinical expertise. AV provides emergency medical response to more than 6 million people across the State of Victoria with AV’s operational area being serviced by strategically placed Ambulance branches. These branches serve all people across 227,000 square kilometres of the State of Victoria. AV also operates an Air Ambulance and Adult Retrieval service who move some of the sickest patients across the state, with locations based at Essendon Airport, Bendigo, Latrobe Valley and Warrnambool.
Health Management Services - Scope of Services 
Ambulance Victoria (AV) is seeking responses from suitably qualified and experienced tenderers, with the intention of establishing a single contractor for the provision of a package of Health Management Services that include, but are not limited to:
a) Employee Health Surveillance Program
• 24-Hour 7-Day Medical Advisory Service
• Monitoring program following an incident (case dependent)
• Post exposure pathology
b) Immunisation Support Program
c) Fitness for Duty
d) Pre-employment Medical Examination
e) Pre-employment Physical Capacity Assessment
f) Medical Consulting Services
• Advice to Infection Control Committee
• Specialist Consulting (as required e.g. during COVID-19)
