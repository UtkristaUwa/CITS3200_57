WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of Education (DoE) is calling for submissions for the Design and Construction of the new A Block Building and associated works located at Belmont State School at 1275 Old Cleveland Road, Carindale, 4152. The new A Block Building consists of 4 No. General Learning Classrooms over 2 levels, inclusive of Staff Area, Collaboration Spaces, Data Room, Amenities and Storerooms. The project also includes a new high-level walkway to connect the existing J Block to the new A Block, new covered access link and pathways, Landscaping, and the demolition of the existing M3 Block and A Block.
    Tender documentation is available electronically from RPS Australia Asia Pacific. Please email your request with your PQC Certificate and company registration details to chris.ray@rpsconsulting.com
    Submissions close on 08 September 2026 at 2:00pm delivered electronically to RPS.rpa.QEDTenders@rpsconsulting.com only.
    Site Visit Details: 10 August 2026 at 10:00am at 1275 Old Cleveland Road, Carindale Qld 4152. * Tenderers are to meet at the School Administration building which is accessed off Scrub Road.
