Description
Traffic Management Services Panel
V/Line Tender Reference VLPRJ1428
Provision of Temporary Traffic Management Services across the network.

In issuing the RFT, V/Line is seeking a panel of contractors to provide Traffic Management Services includes traffic control, equipment hire, planning and permits. Examples of traffic management include;
• VMS board and portable equipment/facility hire (including traffic lights and other site amenities such as toilets, site sheds cameras and etc.)
• Lane closure and detours
• Traffic Control
• Sign Installation
• Road Closures (including road barries; installation and removal)
• Traffic Management and 24 emergency response
• Engineered and non-engineered plans
• Signal Plan
• Obtain approval from either VicRoads, Council or both (e.g. MOA for VicRoads)
• Traffic management plans
Accreditation with Vic Roads is required as mandatory (evidence of certification must be provided). https://transport.vic.gov.au/business/road-and-traffic-management/traffic-management-reform-program/temporary-traffic-management-accreditation-program
Tenderers must be accredited for both Traffic Management Design (TMD) and Traffic Management Implementation (TMI), and must meet the requirements for road Categories 1, 2, and 3.
