WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR or the “Principal”) invites prequalified Tenderers at R3 (Major), F10 (Financial) A2 (Asphalt) and above category to submit a Tender for the construction of the New England Highway (22A Yarraman-Toowoomba) Chalk Drive Cycleway Upgrades.
    This tender will be using the draft revised TIPDS tender process (40% price/60% non-price single stage assessment for tenders valued below $20m).
    The general scope of works is the upgrade of two intersections (Chalk Drive/Ruthven Street and Chalk Drive/Neil Street) to improve continuity of the cycle lanes and improve pedestrian access on the New England Highway. Construction is to be undertaken under traffic on a full-service basis.
    A non-mandatory Briefing and Knowledge Share session will be held via Microsoft Teams (details as per the Conditions of Tendering Annexure) on 23 July 2026 to provide tenderers with a more detailed scope of works as well as the revised tendering process.
    A mandatory pre-lodgement conference will take place at 11:00am on Wednesday 26 August 2026 via Microsoft Teams.
    To obtain documents, eligible prequalified contractors must email: dd_pc@tmr.qld.gov.au to apply for access.
    Once approved, documentation will be made available at the BAS website: https://www.hpw.qld.gov.au/bas/etender/.
    Submissions are to be lodged electronically via the tender site https://www.hpw.qld.gov.au/bas/etender/ prior to 2:00pm on Thursday 10 September 2026.
