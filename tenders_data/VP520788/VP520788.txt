Details:
    The Department of the Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation invites Offers from Suppliers for Invitation to Offer (ITO) No. P253557 for the provision of Queensland Parks and Wildlife Services (QPWS) Fire Protective Clothing.

    Good morning,
    Concerning Part B, Attachment 1 – QPWS Fire Protective Clothing Specifications, item 4. Firefighting Shirt. This garment requirement states "must be labelled with ... AS/NZS 4602.2:2013 Classification 1" - that is not possible as AS/NZS 4602.2 is co-dependent on the garment being certified to AS/NZS 4824 (see AS/NZS 4602.2 Scope and clause 4.5 Garment).
    The illustration shows a reflective tape configuration depicting AS/NZS 1906.4 Class R material with 'Yellow' colour background material (shirt fabric) which would suggest that AS 4602.1:2024 Class D/N Level 1 compliance is possible.
    The shirt design may prevent the garment being AS/NZS 4824 compliant (no Class SP tape specified, business collar, design illustration button front, modesty button).
    Should reference to AS/NZS 4602.2 be replaced with AS 4602.1:2024 Class D/N Level 1?
