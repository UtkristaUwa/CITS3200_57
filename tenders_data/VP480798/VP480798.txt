24/Jul/2026 04:33 PM:
    Question
    :
    Based on historical data can you please provide details regarding emergency campaigns that have occurred at each of the GOC Ports.
    Answered on 05/Aug/2026 08:03 PM
    :
    1. PN response: In the last 15 years emergency dredging has not been enacted for the port managed by Ports North. However extreme weather events (e.g. Cyclone Jasper) or significant siltation occurring in the past has been managed through revised schedules and additional support by the current dredging operator. In 2026 the channel silted up which was addressed through bed-levelling and took 4 days. For Cyclone Jasper (campaign year 2025), the campaign was split across three rounds, for example the first was 6 days (on the way to Weipa), the second was 10 days (on the way back from Weipa and Karumba) and the third was 18 days at the end of the dredging campaign. This was done in consultation with the current dredging operator who dredges these ports, however the scope of this ITO will only include Cairns.
    2. POTL response: In the last 15 years no emergency dredging campaigns have occurred for the Port of Townsville. However extreme weather events have been managed in the past through revised scheduling, for example in 2011 after Cyclone Yasi.
    3. GPC response: In the last 15 years only Bundaberg has required emergency dredging, under the terms of the current contract, due to a declared major flooding event which occurred March 2026 and required 23 days dredging (this included the usual 11 days annual maintenance dredging). Bundaberg also required emergency dredging in 2011 and 2013 following major flooding but depths were restored during the annual planned dredging campaign. Port Alma and Gladstone have not required any emergency dredging.
