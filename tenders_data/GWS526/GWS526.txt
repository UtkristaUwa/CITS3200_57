Description
This Eduction Service Contract (GWS526) requires the supplier to undertake scheduled, reactive and periodic planned eduction services across the Gippsland Water Region. The Supplier will be required to complete eduction services at our facilities such as Wastewater and Water Treatment Plants and on our linear assets such as Air Valve Pit cleaning.
The contract will be for an initial period of three (3) years from the date of execution, with the option of a first extension of two (2) years, and an additional extension of another two (2) years. Therefore, the total contract term is for a maximum period of seven (7) years subject to ongoing satisfactory performance by the Supplier.
