WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    Suppliers are invited to submit an offer for SOA ITO No. QG-PSC2026-4 to provide Executive Recruitment Services for roles classified at the Senior Executive Service (SES) levels SES 2 to SES 4 (and equivalent), as well as Chief Executive (CE) roles.
    The Standing Offer Arrangement (SOA) will consist of three categories of service, as follows:
    1. Executive Recruitment Services
    2. Chief Executive Recruitment Services; and
    3. Services stipulated under “selection and assessment (including scribing services)” component only for roles which are not classified as Senior Executive Service (SES) or Chief Executive (CE) roles.
    Suppliers are required to be willing and able to provide the full, end-to-end suite of services required under the SOA to apply, as specified in Part B: SOA Details

    Pricing for Multiple Vacancies
    The definitions in the SOA Details references a “role” as meaning one or more vacancies at the same Executive Level, advertised at the same time. Schedule 2.1 (Pricing) refers to a percentage fee for each “role”. Does this mean that the same total fee would apply for the recruitment of a role, regardless of the number of individual vacancies/successful placements for that “role”?
    Included Activities for Defined Service Categories
    We wish clarify some of the defined service categories and their included activities, as some of the descriptions are broad and we can see some potential overlap in these. We wondered how this might then affect pricing under the weighted percentage model where a client was not engaging all service categories. For example:
    -	If we were asked to provide an initial longlist/shortlist based only on review of written applications against the role requirements including selection criteria, which service category would that fit into? (it appears to fit in both 2.4 or 2.5)
    -	If we were asked to undertake preliminary screening interviews with selected candidates prior to the selection panel determining a final shortlist, which service category would that fit into? (it appears to fit in either 2.3(d), 2.4, or 2.5)
    Other Common Activities
    There are some other activities which would be required for most recruitment engagements regardless of the nominated categories, but are currently specified in only one (for example, candidate care (2.6 a), administrative support (2.6 b), conflict of interest declarations (2.4 c), provision of final engagement report (2.7 d) and register of candidates (2.7 e)
    When pricing for these activities where there may be some overlap, or where a client has different interpretation of the definitions of the included activities, what is the anticipated approach? Is it expected that we would negotiate pricing with customers, even if that might mean some variation on the approved weighted percentages? For example, if a client wants us to manage candidate care but does not want to engage the “selection and assessment” service category as a whole, would we negotiate a partial percentage given that activity is included in the service category?
    Key Personnel for Service Category 3 (Selection and Assessment Component only)
    Would the requirement for key personnel to be approved for engagement under the SOA also apply to key personnel who would only be engaged to provide lower level scribing services and would not deliver SES/CE Recruitment Services?
