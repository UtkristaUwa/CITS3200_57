SUPPLIER PHISHING ALERT: The Government is aware of an active phishing campaign targeting Victorian Government contractors and suppliers. Cybercriminals are using publicly available procurement information to impersonate Victorian Government officials and send personalised phishing emails. These messages are designed to extract sensitive information and may be used to commit business email compromise or invoice fraud. Learn more about the supplier phishing alert at https://www.buyingfor.vic.gov.au/supplier-phishing-alert

Description
The Department seeks, through this Request for Information (RFI), to gather information from the market on recruitment of domestic and international teachers for government schools via recruitment agencies. This will assist the Department to understand the services and standards, pricing, challenges and innovations used by recruitment agencies sourcing teachers for Australian government schools.
Please see specification document for further information.
