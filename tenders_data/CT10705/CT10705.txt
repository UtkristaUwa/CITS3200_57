Description
North East Water (NEW) invites suitably qualified suppliers to submit a Tender for the supply, delivery, commissioning and training of a Combination Vacuum, Jetting and Hydro Excavation Vehicle.
The vehicle is being procured to establish an in-house vacuum truck capability to support wastewater, water and civil maintenance operations across NEW's service area.
The successful Supplier will provide a fully operational vehicle solution capable of supporting municipal sewer cleaning, pump station cleaning, hydro excavation, emergency response and associated maintenance activities.
The vehicle must be supplied fully assembled, registered and ready for operational use in Victoria.
Operational Requirements
The proposed solution must be capable of supporting the following activities:
Sewer network cleaning;
Sewer blockage response;
Emergency sewer overflow response;
Pump station cleaning;
Wet well cleaning;
Treatment plant cleaning and desludging;
Hydro excavation and non-destructive digging;
Water and sewer asset exposure;
Environmental incident response;
Civil construction and maintenance support; and
Emergency operational support activities.
The vehicle must comply with all requirements specified in the Functional Requirements Specification (FRS) contained in Part 3A of this Request for Tender.
Respondents should refer to the Tender documentation for the Functional Requirements Specification, evaluation criteria, key procurement dates and other requirements relevant to this procurement.
Any enquiries regarding this Request for Tender (RFT) must be directed to North East Water Procurement at procurement@newater.com.au, referencing CT10705.
