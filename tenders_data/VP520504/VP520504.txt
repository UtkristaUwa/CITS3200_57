WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The State of Queensland through Queensland Health (the Principal), invites you to submit a Tender for the provision of a suitably qualified civil contractor to construct the Enabling Works for the Cooktown MPHS Redevelopment in accordance with the Invitation documents.
    The Queensland Government is investing $200 million to redevelop the Cooktown Multipurpose Health Service (CMPHS), improving access to care for Cooktown and surrounding communities.
    Health Infrastructure Queensland in partnership with Torres and Cape Hospital and Health Service are responsible for delivery of the project as part of the Hospital Rescue Plan. The project will deliver a range of essential healthcare facilities and services to meet the needs of the community and reduce the need for patients to travel outside the region.
    The scope of this request is for an enabling works package the Project team have identified to mitigate risks to the main construction which is planned in 2027.
    For detailed information on the specific goods / services sought under this Request for Offer, please review the "Request for Offer" documentation.
