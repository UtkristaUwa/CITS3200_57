Description
Request for Tender - Asset Storage and Transport Services for the Sheriff's Office Victoria
1. Overview of services sought
The Sheriff’s Office Victoria (SOV) is responsible for actioning warrants in Victoria and enforcing sanctions against those who do not comply with court orders in accordance with the Sheriff Act 2009. The sanctions available to Sheriff’s officers to enforce civil and criminal warrants includes the seizure and sale of assets (which includes vehicles and chattels).
To support this, SOV currently outsources the following services (the Services) under an existing contract:
Transport of vehicles and chattels from seizure locations (within both metropolitan and regional Victoria) to the Contractor’s storage facility
Receiving vehicles and chattels into the Contractor’s storage facility
Long-term storage of vehicles and chattels, as well as personal property found in vehicles
Preparation of vehicles and chattels for auctioning
Release (i.e. handover) of vehicles and chattels to the person or entity named in a Sheriff’s warrant
Provision of documentation and reporting associated with the Services to SOV.
With the current contract for the Services approaching the end of its term, SOV is undertaking a competitive tender process for the Services to ensure service continuity for SOV.
SOV wishes to engage a single supplier with the capacity to deliver the required Services on an end-to-end basis. This must be as a prime contractor delivering all the services directly, or with identified services delivered by subcontractors.
