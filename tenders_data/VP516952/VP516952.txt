WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Queensland Parliamentary Service (QPS) is located within the Parliamentary Precinct at corner George and Alice Streets, Brisbane, and is also responsible for 93 electorates located throughout Queensland from which each Member for Parliament conducts the business of their respective electorate.
    QPS are calling for submissions from Security Contractors to supply and install electronic security & access control systems for a statewide upgrade to the Electorate Office security systems.
    Please read the attached documents for further details.
