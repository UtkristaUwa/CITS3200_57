Description
The Office of the Victorian Government Architect (OVGA) invites expressions of interest from highly experienced built environment professionals to join their pool of State Design Review (SDR) experts.
OVGA is seeking recognised leaders with extensive project delivery experience across architecture, engineering, landscape architecture, planning, urban design and specialist disciplines including sustainability, accessibility, heritage, health and other relevant built environment professions.
SDR experts contribute independent, specialist advice to support high-quality design outcomes across a diverse range of significant projects and Victorian Government initiatives. This is an opportunity to apply your professional expertise to improve the quality, sustainability and liveability of Victoria's built environment.
To apply to be an expert member of OVGA’s State Design Review pool, please register as a supplier https://www.tenders.vic.gov.au/register then download the 2026 OVGA State Design Review - Nomination process for new applicants and follow the application instructions.
Applications close at 5.00 pm on 1st September 2026.

PLEASE NOTE:  You must register as a 'Supplier' in order to download the attached documents and/or submit an expression of interest.  You may register as an individual by entering your personal name in the "Business Name" and "Legal Name" field leaving the ABN field blank where you do not have one.  You are not required to choose a Product/Services Category in order to complete registration.
