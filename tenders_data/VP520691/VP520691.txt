    Question
    :
    Good morning,
    In Part D – Schedule of Prices under “Termite Treatment”, it asks for confirmation that employee(s) hold a QBCC Termite Management – Chemical and a QBCC Termite Management – Physical licence. However, the services listed only require a QBCC Termite Management – Chemical licence.
    Could you please confirm whether both licences are required, or only Chemical licensing for this tender?
    Answered on 12/Aug/2026 01:04 PM
    :
    (edited on 13/Aug/2026 10:58 AM)
    Thank you for your enquiry.
    Tenderers are required to hold both a QBCC Termite Management – Chemical licence and a QBCC Termite Management – Physical licence.
    While the termite treatment services currently listed in the Schedule of Rates primarily require a Termite Management – Chemical licence, the contract may include other termite management works that require a Termite Management – Physical licence. As subcontracting is not permitted under this arrangement, suppliers must be appropriately licensed to undertake the full range of potential termite-related services that may arise during the contract term.
    Accordingly, the requirement for both licence types remains unchanged, and tenderers should ensure they can demonstrate compliance with both licensing requirements in their submission.
    Please also note.
    The QBCC Termite Management - Physical licence (along with the QBCC Termite Management – Chemical Licence) is only required if offering for the termite treatments, which is optional under this trade. The mandatory requirement for this SOA is the Pest Management Licence issued by Qld Health, which is required to undertake the basic and additional treatments etc.
