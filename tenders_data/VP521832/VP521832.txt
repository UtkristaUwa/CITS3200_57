WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The purpose of the EOI Document is to set out the Procurement Process, EOI Conditions, the Evaluation Process, the Principal's objectives, and key details that the Respondent needs to know to submit a conforming Submission.
    The scope includes the removal of existing structures within the proposed footprint of the main works, isolation of services, and removal of excess fill from site. The Early Works Contractor will be responsible for the planning, design, and delivery of deconstruction and demolition works, earthworks, and associated activities, including utility disconnections and site delineation.
    This includes demolition of the existing western grandstand, plaza pavements and redundant services, partial site levelling, stockpiling of suitable material, and the removal of approximately 17,000 m³ of material from the eastern mound.
    The Early Works Contractor will provide all labour, plant, materials, temporary works, supervision, permits, approvals, subcontractors and services necessary to deliver the Project in a safe, compliant, and coordinated manner.
    Key activities include:
    •	establishment of site access and security;
    •	demolition;
    •	site clearing and protection;
    •	bulk earthworks;
    •	utility management;
    •	surface water management;
    •	traffic management;
    •	erosion and sediment controls;
    •	environmental controls; and
    •	technical reporting and documentation.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Sunshine Coast Stadium (SCS) is a major regional sporting and events facility that supports community participation, high-performance sport and the delivery of large-scale events.
    The SCS has been identified as a host venue for 2032 Brisbane Olympic and Paralympic Games, necessitating upgrades to meet Games requirements and support increased future demand.
    The Sunshine Coast’s home of big events will be bigger and better with an upgrade to SCS, securing its position as the region’s premier venue for local, national and international sporting, leisure and entertainment events.
    The stadium upgrade will boost permanent seating with a new eastern grandstand and a rebuilt western grandstand. Upgrades will include multi-purpose community spaces and amenities accessible to people of all abilities.
    The early design vision by awarded Principal Consultant, Architectus, is inspired by the Sunshine Coast’s natural landscape and coastal lifestyle. Initial concepts explore light, open forms, shaded concourses and elevated seating to create a relaxed, immersive game-day experience.
    The sports fields at SCS support a range of activities, from grassroots community sport to elite training camps and national-level football, rugby league, and rugby union games.
    The planned upgrades will deliver modern, high-quality facilities for athletes, officials, and fans, ensuring the venue continues to serve the region for decades to come.
    The SCS will be owned and operated by Sunshine Coast Council (SCC), delivering a lasting legacy for the region through enhanced tourism, economic development, and increased opportunities for community engagement and active recreation.
