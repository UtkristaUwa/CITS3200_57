WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of the Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation invites Offers from eligible Suppliers for the provision of services to support the North and Far North Tropical Low Environmental Recovery Program, Catchment Rehabilitation Program.
    The scope of this Invitation to Offer (ITO) focusses on the delivery of projects across 40 activated local government areas to support the recovery of catchments impacted by the North and Far North Tropical Low, 29 January – 28 February 2025 event, improving condition and building resilience in the impacted environments and associated ecological processes.
    Eligibility - Please note that Suppliers and projects offered through this ITO process are required to meet eligibility requirements outlined within Part A - Invitation to Offer, Attachment C - Eligibility Details. Please ensure to carefully review this information prior to submitting your offer.
    Supplier Briefing Session - A Supplier Briefing Session will be held at 2.00pm on 8 September 2026 for this ITO. Please refer to Part A - Invitation to Offer, Section 1.5 - Briefing Session for details to register.
