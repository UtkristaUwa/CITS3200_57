WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    Energy Queensland Limited (EQL) is seeking proposals for Meter to Cash Solution and Implementation Services via SAP Ariba.
    Tenderers who wish to submit a response need to be registered as a Supplier on SAP Ariba. For more information, please see below.
    EQL is seeking implementation of a Meter to Cash solution that is cloud-native, highly scalable and compliant with Australian industry standards and regulatory requirements, particularly those set by AEMO and other industry bodies.
    Part A: Product
    The Meter to Cash Solution must be capable of supporting both Ergon Energy and Energex entities within a single instance, with company-specific business processes, configurations, and workflows where necessary, while also enabling shared processes where appropriate.
    Tenders must comply with the following mandatory criteria:
    - Australian Energy Market Capability
    • Proposed solution is deployed in production within an Australian DNSP operating in the National Electricity Market (NEM).
    - Product Capability: Cloud-hosted solution that provides the following core capabilities:
    • 1.0 Meter Data Management (MDM).
    • 2.0 Network Billing Management (NBM+CIS).
    • 3.0 Market Transactions Management (MDM +CIS).
    • 4.0 Connection Point Management (CIS)
    - Local Presence and Support Capability
    • Australian-based support model.
    • Local functional, technical and customer success resources.
    - Cyber Security
    • Hosted in Australia.
    • ISO27001 Certified.
    • SOC2 Type 2 Annual Assurance (or equivalent).
    Part B: Implementation Services
    The Solution will be implemented through a series of controlled delivery Stages and production releases.
    • Stage 1: High Level Design – Business Process Gap Analysis, High Level Design and Detailed Implementation Plan (as defined in SOW1)
    • Stage 2: Detailed Design and Build (as defined in SOW2)
    • Stage 3: Systems Integration Testing (as defined in SOW2)
    • Stage 4: UAT & Cutover Readiness (as defined in SOW2)
    • Stage 5: Go-Live, Hypercare & Warranty (as defined in SOW2)
    Tenders must comply with the following mandatory criteria.
    - Product Experience: Tenderer must have proven experience implementing Meter-to-Cash solution(s) that meet Part A mandatory criteria.
    - Australian Utility Experience: Tenderer must have implementation experience within the Australian Utilities market.
