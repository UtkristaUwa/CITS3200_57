Description

Parks Victoria is seeking to engage a panel for 2 regions (Gippsland and North East Victoria) of suitably qualified suppliers under a Deed of Standing Offer to deliver ground shooting of deer and pest animals including feral pigs, foxes, cats and goats to protect critical high value environmental and cultural assets including endangered and vulnerable species and vegetation communities.

The project must have the ability to adapt to changes in circumstances which may impact operational commitments and will be dependent on funding availability.

The Deed of Standing Offer will be established for a 3 year period with optional terms to extend the services by 2x1 year periods.

Parks Victoria intends, at its own discretion, to issue supply orders for the carrying out of specific services throughout the contract duration. The services required may be ad-hoc over the contract term with periods of high and low activity depending on the operational requirements and funding availability.
