WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    The Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR) is seeking applications for new applicants to join the BHTSP Contract Administration Panel.
    This application process is only for new applicants wishing to join the BHTSP Contract Administration Panel (existing BHTSP Contract Administration Panel Members are not permitted to apply for this process).
    The Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR) is delivering the Bruce Highway Targeted Safety Program (BHTSP). The Bruce Highway is a vital 1,673 kilometre north-south corridor in Queensland, connecting Brisbane to Cairns. In January 2025, the Australian and Queensland governments announced a joint funding commitment of $9 billion towards the BHTSP to enhance safety and uplift deficient sections of the highway to achieve a three-star rating.
    The BHTSP focuses on sections of the Bruce Highway between Gympie and Cairns with a high safety risk.
    Projects to be delivered through the BHTSP will be awarded to prequalified contractors and prequalified on the Construction Panel. The BHTSP will be delivered in the following districts:
    - Far North Queensland District
    - Fitzroy District
    - Mackay/Whitsunday District
    - Northern District
    - Wide Bay/Burnett District
    Contract Administration Panel Members may be engaged to deliver a project under either a:
    - Transport Infrastructure Contract – Construct Only (BHTSP TIC-CO Contract) (Construct only contract); or
    - Transport Infrastructure Contract – Target Cost (TIC-TC Contract) (Design and Construct contract),

BACKGROUND INFORMATION / COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    To be eligible to apply to join the Contract Administration Panel, the Applicant must be prequalified under the Infrastructure Building and Construction Panel (IBCP) in the following categories:
    • Contract Administrator (CA)
    • Inspector (Roadworks) (IR)
    • Inspector (Structures) (IS)
    • Site Engineer (Civil) (SEC)
    • Design Engineer (Civil) (DEC)
    • Designer Technical Advisor (DT)
    Panel Applicants are not required to possess every IBCP prequalification category listed above, but as a minimum the company must have CA01, IR01, and SEC01.
