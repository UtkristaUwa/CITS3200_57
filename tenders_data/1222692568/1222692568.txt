Project overview
V/Line has an ongoing requirement for specialist rail infrastructure services across the Victorian Country Rail Network to support its capital works and maintenance programs.
V/Line is seeking proposals from suitably qualified and experienced Tenderers for the provision of Track Maintenance and Rail Surface Improvement services. The services sought under this Request for Tender (RFT) comprise the following categories:
Maintenance Tamping Services
Rail Milling Services
Rail Grinding Services
Tenderers may submit proposals for one or more of the above service categories.
The successful Tenderer(s) will be required to demonstrate the capability, experience, resources, safety performance, and technical expertise necessary to deliver the relevant services across the Victorian Country Rail Network in accordance with V/Line's operational, safety, and performance requirements.
This procurement aims to establish reliable service arrangements that support the ongoing maintenance, renewal, and optimisation of V/Line's rail infrastructure assets, ensuring safe, efficient, and sustainable railway operations.
