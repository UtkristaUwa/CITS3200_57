WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    Racing Queensland’s (RQ) Registered Training Organisation is seeking offers from suitably qualified suppliers for an integrated Student Management System (SMS) and Learning Management System (LMS). RQ's preference is to engage a single supplier under a single contract for the provision, implementation, integration and ongoing support of the solution. Where a proposed solution comprises multiple products or third-party components, the supplier must assume end-to-end responsibility for delivery and ongoing support and provide a single point of accountability.
    The successful Tenderer will be engaged via the QITC General Contract Conditions to complete the works.

    Mandatory criterion (f) requires evidence of successful implementation within at least two Australian RTOs of comparable size, while Schedule A section 2.8 asks for experience with "Registered Training Organisations (RTOs) or comparable organisations". Where a vendor's implementations to date are with comparable Australian government and public-safety organisations (including live, production-equivalent evaluation environments operating at a substantially larger scale than this engagement) rather than two completed commercial RTO contracts, will the Customer accept, as evidence against criterion (f): (a) listing on the NCVER AVETMISS Compliant Software Register with AVS-validated NAT export; (b) those comparable-organisation implementations and evaluations; and (c) hands-on evaluator access to a fully populated tenant, so the evaluation panel can verify capability directly rather than by reference alone?
    Answered on 04/Aug/2026 02:05 PM
    :
    Thank you for your question.
    No. The mandatory criterion is to remain unchanged. Racing Queensland has included this requirement to minimise implementation and operational risk associated with procuring a business-critical LMS/SMS. While we acknowledge the alternative evidence proposed, including AVETMISS compliance, implementations within comparable organisations, and access to a live demonstration environment, this does not satisfy the requirement for evidence of successful implementation within at least two Australian Registered Training Organisations (RTOs) of comparable size. As this system will underpin our student management, training delivery, compliance and regulatory reporting functions, Racing Queensland requires the assurance that comes from proven implementation experience in comparable Australian RTO environments.

This criterion will remain unchanged and will be applied consistently to all respondents.
