WHAT THE BUYER IS REQUESTING:
----------------------------------------
Details:
    Provision of soil nailing for Mungar Road Batter Slip
    Upslope:
    1)	Descaling the face. Strip topsoil & clear vegetation as required.
    2)	Supply & install soil nails, including grout & weepholes.
    3)	Shotcreting including supply & installation of mesh.
    Downslope:
    4)	Strip topsoil & clear vegetation.
    5)	General excavation & disposal of existing batter material. Excavation includes the removal of existing culvert end unit(s).
    6)	Supply & install lower embankment.
    7)	Supply & install 1/450 RCP culvert extension.
    8)	Supply & install upper embankment.
    9)	Supply & install soil nails, including grout & weepholes.
    10)	Shotcreting including supply & installation of mesh.
